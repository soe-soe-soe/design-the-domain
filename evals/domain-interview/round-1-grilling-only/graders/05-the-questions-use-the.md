The questions of round 1 never use one of these words for a deadline or its parts: "Termin" on its own, "Deadline", "Ablaufdatum", "Zuständiger", "Owner" (the Avoid entries of the Fristen glossary). "Termin" inside the glossary terms Projekttermin, Persönlicher Termin and Zieltermin is fine, and so is any word that only appears in a quotation of the requirement.
