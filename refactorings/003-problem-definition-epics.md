# Refactoring 003: Epics in problem-definition

Status: open · Skill: problem-definition

Open question: does the problem space need a level above the story?

Today a session turns one feature idea into several stories and their FRs; nothing groups them.
BMAD has epics as an envelope with outcome, boundaries and done-when checks, refined into
requirements and sliced into stories in build order.

## For epics

- A feature that spans several stories gets one place for its goal and its boundaries.
- Build order and dependencies between stories become explicit.
- "Feature done" becomes checkable, not only "story done".

## Against epics

- FRs already hold the target state; an epic adds a third artefact to keep in sync.
- The session that started the stories is the grouping today; that may be enough.
- Another level means more status and more open points to track.

## To decide

- Whether epics are needed at all, or a session reference in the stories is enough.
- If needed: which artefact, who writes it, and whether it carries open points.
